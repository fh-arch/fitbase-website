# Measurement Plan

## Primary conversion

- Demo intent: click from a commercial page to `/demo`
- Demo handoff: click from `/demo` to WhatsApp or email

## Acquisition dimensions

- Landing page and commercial topic
- Referrer domain
- Standard UTM parameters
- AI referral indicators, including `utm_source=chatgpt.com` when provided

## Search and answer-engine monitoring

- Google Search Console: indexed pages, queries, impressions, clicks
- Bing Webmaster Tools: index and query coverage
- Server logs: verified search/AI crawler requests, status codes and crawlable page delivery
- Monthly manual citation checks using stable, neutral questions across ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity and Gemini

Do not claim that crawl permission guarantees inclusion, ranking or citation. Record the question, date, model/product, answer and cited URL for each manual check.
