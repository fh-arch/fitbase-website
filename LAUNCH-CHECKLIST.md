# Launch Checklist and 30/60/90 Plan

## Before deployment

- [ ] Build passes from the release commit
- [ ] No unrelated local files are staged
- [ ] Product owner approves PRODUCT-TRUTH.md
- [ ] Legal owner reviews the three legal pages and subprocessors
- [ ] Cloudflare cache purge is available after deployment

## After deployment

- [ ] Smoke-test home, hubs, all feature/solution pages and legal pages
- [ ] Check robots, sitemap, llms and canonical redirects
- [ ] Submit sitemap to Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools
- [ ] Request indexing for the home, feature hub and four solution pages

## 30 days

- Record baseline indexed pages, queries, conversions and crawler hits.
- Publish two B2B buyer guides grounded in verified product capabilities.
- Resolve crawl, duplicate-title and structured-data warnings.

## 60 days

- Add verified case evidence with written customer permission.
- Publish comparison/evaluation content without invented competitor claims.
- Improve pages using real query and conversion data.

## 90 days

- Review AI citation observations across ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity and Gemini.
- Refresh weak or stale pages; expand only topics with verified product relevance.
- Re-audit bot policies, legal disclosures and product truth after releases.
